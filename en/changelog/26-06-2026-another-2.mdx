## Third Release

Now you can drill down by make, then model, then edition. Each step narrows the field, so you only see options relevant to where you are—fewer entries to scan, and less chance of picking the wrong one.

### Smarter, traffic-aware navigation
Navigation now calculates and recalculates routes more accurately, and factors in live traffic. You get faster, more reliable routes that adjust as conditions change.

### Real-time station tiles
The tile service now updates charge stations faster and more accurately, so what you see is always in sync with the actual station.
