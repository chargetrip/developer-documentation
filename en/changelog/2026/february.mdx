## Driving styles

![Driving styles](../../../assets/changelog/2026/february.jpg)

Driving speed directly affects EV energy consumption. To make routes more accurately reflect real-world vehicle behavior and trip duration, we are introducing two new controls: speed factor and driving styles.

Driving styles change estimated arrival time and energy consumption.

- **Eco** assumes a more relaxed, efficient pace to save battery;
- **Sport** assumes higher speeds and greater consumption for a quicker arrival.

These features allow you to adjust routing calculations based on how a vehicle is actually driven, resulting in more precise consumption estimates and arrival times.


### Updated getRouteStationsWithAmenities Schema

We updated the `getRouteStationsWithAmenities` query to simplify inputs and support multi-amenity filtering.

- **Multi-amenity search**: Query multiple amenity categories simultaneously with logical OR filtering;
- **Direct time inputs**: Pass time windows directly in seconds from the route origin;
- **External station id**: Access `external_id` on the station nodes.
