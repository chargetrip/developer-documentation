title: How connector filtering works
slug: station-pricing-evse-connector
fieldName: StationPricesFilter.evse_connector
variant: tooltip
position: top
---
Only return the prices that apply to the given EVSE and connector pairs, with a maximum of 10 pairs. Use the `uid` of an EVSE and the `id` of one of its connectors, both available in the [station query](/api-reference/core/station/query-station-details).
