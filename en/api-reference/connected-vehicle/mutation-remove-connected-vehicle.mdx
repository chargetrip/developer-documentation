---
title: Remove connected vehicle
schemaName: removeConnectedVehicle
schemaType: Mutation
---

Remove a connected vehicle and revoke its access by using the removeConnectedVehicle mutation. This is useful when an end-user disconnects their vehicle from the connectivity provider.

## Mutation

An `id` is required to remove a connected vehicle. The `id` can be obtained through the [createConnectedVehicle mutation](/api-reference/connected-vehicle/mutation-create-connected-vehicle).
