Route emissions provide a detailed breakdown of the greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions a vehicle produces during a trip, taking into account the embedded, operational and end-of-life scopes. Emissions can be retrieved for any route calculated with the Chargetrip API. They are expressed in grams of carbon dioxide equivalent (gCO₂eq). Component-level data lets you build up your own emissions summary.

A more detailed description of the method can be found in [this blog post](https://www.chargetrip.com/newsroom/abstract-a-generalized-method-for-estimating-comprehensive-emissions-for-electric-vehicles-on-a-per-trip-basis). For additional details, [request the white paper](https://www.chargetrip.com/newsroom/abstract-a-generalized-method-for-estimating-comprehensive-emissions-for-electric-vehicles-on-a-per-trip-basis#download-paper).

## Scopes

Embedded
: The share of emissions produced when the vehicle was made. This includes material acquisition, transport and production of components, assembly of the vehicle and battery, and initial vehicle distribution.

Operational
: The direct and indirect emissions that result from driving the vehicle over the route. The main component is the production, distribution and consumption of fuel or electricity. Other components are vehicle maintenance and road infrastructure. The estimate takes into account the projected demand on the energy grid, alongside the local climate and season.

End-of-life
: The share of emissions that will be produced when the vehicle is decommissioned, for the given route. This includes the disposal of the vehicle's major components and body.
