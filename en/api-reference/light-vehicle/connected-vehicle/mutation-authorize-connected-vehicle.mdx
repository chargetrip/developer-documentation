Complete the authorization of a connected vehicle by using the authorizeConnectedVehicle mutation. This is useful after the end-user has granted access through the connectivity provider.

## Mutation

An `id` and provider-specific `options` are required to authorize a connected vehicle. The `id` can be obtained through the [createConnectedVehicle mutation](/api-reference/light-vehicle/connected-vehicle/mutation-create-connected-vehicle).
