## Experimental and deprecation API warnings

![Warnings changelog image](../../../assets/changelog/july-2026/warnings.png)

We have introduced lifecycle warnings in GraphQL response extensions to inform developers whenever a requested operation or field is in Alpha, Beta, or scheduled for deprecation. This provides direct visibility into feature maturity and upcoming deprecations without disrupting API responses.

What’s new:

- **Lifecycle notifications**: Receive structured alerts under `extensions.warnings` whenever your request touches schema elements marked with `@experimental(stage: "alpha")`, `@experimental(stage: "beta")`, or `@chargetripdeprecated`;
- **Consistent delivery**: Warnings are included across all responses regardless of whether the operation succeeds or returns errors.

### Fixes & improvements

<details>
    <summary>Improvements</summary>

    - Extended `createIsoline` to support custom weather conditions (`temperature`, `air_pressure`, and `solar_irradiance`) alongside standard presets (`summer`, `winter`, `actual`) via the new `weather` input, deprecating the `season` input.
    - Added station operating status warnings at the route leg level (`station_closed`, `station_closing_soon`, and `station_unknown_open_hours`) and refined `station_limited_open_hours` to warn drivers when a station on their route may be closed or have limited/unknown operating hours upon arrival.
    - Standardized schema annotations by replacing hardcoded `[BETA]` text descriptions with the formal `@experimental(stage: "beta")` directive across the Isoline, Routing V2, and Navigation GraphQL schemas.
</details>

<details>
    <summary>Fixes</summary>

    - Fixed an issue in getRoute where savings.money returned null instead of 0 on BEV routes when no monetary savings were accrued.
</details>
