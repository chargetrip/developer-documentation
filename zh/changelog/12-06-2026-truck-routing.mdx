## EV truck routing, built for the long haul

![Warnings changelog image](../../../assets/changelog/july-2026/warnings.png)

Planning a truck route used to mean stitching it together yourself. One map for the roads, the rulebook for your driving hours, a separate search for somewhere to stop and somewhere to charge.

Now EV truck routing does it in a single pass. It keeps you on roads built for your vehicle, works your EU driving and rest rules into the plan so your hours stay compliant, finds sensible overnight stops, and picks out the best chargers along the way. One route that already accounts for everything; fewer tools to check, and less chance of a plan that falls apart on the road.
